Run #46 – Karl the Fog, a Big Boat Regatta, and Empanadas on Angel Island

Tagline: Third half marathon, third time on the island, and this time the whole family came along for a bike ride.

Run number 46 went back to a familiar favorite: Angel Island, now for the third time and the third half marathon logged there in the 52@52 challenge. This time, though, the trip wasn't organized around my running — my wife put together the day, a bike ride around the island with friends and family, and I simply used the occasion to sneak in run 46 on an island that keeps proving itself to be an amazing place to run, with that perfect mix of paved and unpaved trail plus a solid dose of elevation.

Fog, sun, and Karl doing his thing

The day started foggy and gray, but it opened up into a genuinely beautiful, sunny afternoon. The timing worked out perfectly: cool enough at the start to run comfortably, occasionally a bit chilly when the wind picked up, but never uncomfortable. The shifting weather also gave me a completely different set of views compared to my last two visits — watching Karl the Fog roll in off the ocean through the Golden Gate Bridge and spill over the hills of Sausalito is one of those sights that never gets old, and this time I got to see it from angles I hadn't caught before.

A bay full of sailboats

The water below was busy with sailboats, more than I'd typically expect, and it looked very much like a race. Several boats large enough to carry ten or more crew were moving fast and close together, with everyone leaning out on one side to balance against the wind — a proper racing formation. It turns out the timing lined up with the 2026 Rolex Big Boat Series, hosted by the St. Francis Yacht Club, which ran September 16–20 on San Francisco Bay and doubled as the ORC North American Championship, drawing 81 boats across five classes [web:63][web:67]. That would explain the fleet of big, fast, tightly packed boats I saw racing past the island.

Empanadas, pastries, and the gang

After wrapping up the run, I met up with Paula and the rest of the group who had spent the day biking around the island. The reward for everyone was well earned: empanadas from El Porteño Empanadas and pastries from Parachute, both excellent choices to close out a day that mixed running, biking, fog, sun, and sailboats all in one place.

Audiobook and shoes: staying consistent

I'm still working through The Dog Stars on audiobook, now down to the last hour before finishing it, and I ran again in my Altra Wild 2, which continues to be my go-to shoe for these trail-and-pavement mixes.

Run #46 done. Third half marathon on Angel Island, a front-row seat to Karl the Fog's best moves, a big boat regatta offshore, and a very good excuse for empanadas afterward.

See you next time!
