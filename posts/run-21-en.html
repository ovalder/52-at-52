Run #21 – Spring Heat on the Stevens Creek Trail

📅 2026-04-04

Back on a new (to the challenge) trail

Run #21 took me to the Stevens Creek Trail, a place I already knew but hadn’t yet used for the 52@52 challenge. It’s one of those trails that feels made for runners and cyclists: clean, well maintained, and with that mix of nature and urban infrastructure that keeps things interesting as the miles go by.

Paula on the bike, version 2.0

Once again, I had Paula on the bike as my support crew, and that makes a huge difference on days like this. Knowing she’s right there with me, ready to help if I need to stop, turn around, or just grab some water, takes a bit of the mental load off the run and makes the whole outing feel lighter, even when the legs disagree.

Beautiful day, late start, and heat

The day itself was beautiful: blue skies, bright light, and the kind of spring weather that looks perfect in photos but starts to feel a lot less friendly once the temperature climbs. We started a little later than we should have, and by the second half of the run it was definitely on the hot side for my current fitness and recovery level. I ended up walking more in that second half, using those breaks to cool down and reset instead of trying to push through and paying for it later.

Water on wheels

One of the best decisions of the day was having Paula carry a bottle of water for me on the bike. On hot days, the water in my backpack warms up pretty quickly, but a separate bottle stays cooler for longer and is much easier to access during short walk breaks. Being able to grab that bottle, pour some water, and actually feel refreshed made the second half of the run possible, even with the extra heat.
