Run #47 – Solo Miles at Quicksilver, One Lens Short of 360

Tagline: Shaded trails, a shoulder-mounted camera experiment, and only five runs left to go.

Run number 47 took me back to Almaden Quicksilver County Park, this time solo. My wife couldn't join me on the bike today, but training can't wait — the Lake Chabot 50K trail run in November isn't going to run itself, and every trail with real elevation between now and then counts.

A late, cool, shaded start

I got a late start on this one, but that ended up working in my favor. The morning stayed cool, and the trail offered shade almost the entire way, which made the later start far more forgiving than it could have been on a hotter day.

Testing a new way to carry the 360 camera

This run doubled as a gear experiment — and, unfortunately, a documentation failure. I tried a new way to mount my Insta360 X5 on my running vest, clipping it onto the vertical strap that runs over the shoulder instead of holding it in my hand. The upside is real: the camera stays solidly in place and leaves my hands free for the whole run. The tradeoff is that this mounting only lets me use one of the two lenses, so I lose the true 360-degree capture — a price to pay for the convenience.

I also tried controlling the camera from an app on my Google Pixel Watch. Starting the recording appeared to work, but stopping it from the watch did not, so I assumed the camera was recording for the preset duration and would shut down automatically. After checking everything later, though, I found out that no video had been recorded at all.

To make it worse, I did not take any photos with my phone because I thought the camera was doing its job. So run #47 ended up being the most poorly documented run of the entire 52@52 challenge so far. At least it was a useful test — now I know I need to verify that the camera is actually recording before trusting it on another trail run.

New audiobook: Bag of Bones

I wrapped up The Dog Stars and moved on to Bag of Bones by Stephen King. Ironically, the Dog Stars movie left theaters before I got a chance to see it, so that comparison between book and film will have to wait until it turns up on streaming.

Yesterday I also squeezed in 6 miles, since I need to start stacking up more distance between now and November for Lake Chabot. While running, I came across a line in Bag of Bones that fit this challenge perfectly: “Cross each bridge when you come to it is the motto of champions.” To me, that means one race at a time. And now, with 47 down, there are only five half marathons left to go.
