Run #40 – Three-Peat by the Creek

📅 2026-08-08

Same trail, favorite shoes, late heat, and a few strategically scheduled walks.

Run 40 brought Paula and me back to the Los Gatos Creek Trail — Paula on the bike, me on my feet — for the third run in a row. I think this is an official 52@52 record. At this point, the creek probably recognizes us, and the bridges may be wondering why we keep coming back.

Data from my Google Pixel Watch 4 view on a computer via the Fitness Dashboard website:

The Altra Torin 8s joined me again and have quickly become my favorite shoes. After taking a brand-new pair straight into a half marathon last week, I probably should have been more cautious. Instead, they felt great again, so I am now prepared to give them completely undeserved credit for every good run I have from here on.

We started really late this time, which meant the weather was already deep into its “let’s see what these runners are made of” phase. The heat caught up with me near the end, and I had to walk a few times. But that was not suffering — it was advanced race strategy, also known as moving forward while pretending the shade is a hydration station.

Due to the late start, we did something different before the run: we had breakfast. Because of that, I skipped my traditional Huma gel during the run. I only had water with Nuun electrolytes, carried by Paula in a bottle on her bike.

I’m still listening to This Is Why We Lied, and it continues to be properly intriguing. I keep expecting the next chapter to reveal something dramatic just as I’m trying to negotiate with the sun. More mystery, more creek miles, and hopefully a little less late-afternoon heat next time.

Run 40 is checked off: third consecutive visit to the same trail, Paula still cruising beside me, Torin 8s still winning, and one more half-marathon added to the list.

Only 12 to go!
