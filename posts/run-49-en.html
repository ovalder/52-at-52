Run #49 – Ocean Views, Lava Rocks, and a Family Finish in Maui

Tagline: Chasing ocean spray, cooling off at beach showers, and finishing with family and a dip in the ocean.

Run number 49 took the 52@52 challenge somewhere special: Maui, Hawaii. I ran as close to the ocean as I could, finishing at Wailea Beach, where my wife and daughter were waiting. With only a few runs left, this was a memorable place to check off another half marathon.

Heat, lava rocks, and beach showers

It was pretty hot, but some shade along the route and partly cloudy skies during the second half helped. I still walked a few small inclines and the stretches over lava rocks near the water — slowing down to watch my footing and get closer to the splashing waves was worth it.

A couple of stops at beach showers to soak my hat and neck cover also made a welcome difference. After the walk-in fridge during Run #48, my improvised cooling stations are getting much better scenery!

Plenty of scenery, not many “Alohas”

People were friendly and made room when they saw me approaching. Surprisingly, nobody responded to my “Aloha” greetings. I thought I was being friendly and cordial, but apparently my running “Aloha” wasn't catching on! No idea why, but people were considerate all the same.

I skipped the audiobook this time. With those views, birds calling, and waves breaking nearby, I wanted to soak in the island rather than follow a story. I also used my Insta360 X5 along the way — check out the short video compilation for some of the best moments.

The best finish: family and the ocean

Reaching Wailea Beach and finding my wife and daughter waiting was a great finish. Getting into the ocean afterward made it even better: a welcome cooldown, even if the water wasn't exactly cold. After a hot run along the coast, finally getting into the water felt just right.

Run #49 in the books. Ocean views, lava rocks, beach showers, and a family finish — only three half marathons left in the 52@52 challenge.

See you next time!
