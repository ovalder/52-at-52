Run #50 – New Island, More Hills, and a RunGo Reminder in Kauai

Tagline: A beach start, mountain views, a few hills, and another ocean-splash finish — only two to go.

Run number 50 stayed in Hawaii, but moved to a new island: Kauai. Once again, I used the RunGo app to find a route, just as I had in Maui. I started at beautiful Poipu Beach, where my wife and daughter spent their time while I headed out for what turned into a hot, hilly 13.62 miles.

A useful app — and the headphones I forgot I needed

In Maui, I wore my Anker C50i open-ear headphones and got voice directions whenever there was a turn. That was quite useful for following an unfamiliar route without constantly checking my phone.

This time, I left the headphones behind because I wasn't planning to listen to an audiobook. What I forgot was that they could also be useful for navigation! RunGo announced mileage and pace through the phone speaker, but I didn't get turn directions. I'm not sure whether that was something about the route, my settings, or the audio setup — something to figure out.

Either way, based on these two outings, I strongly recommend RunGo for finding new places to run. It has been especially useful while away from home.

Less coastline, more hills

This route wasn't quite as scenic as the one in Maui, mainly because it didn't follow the coast. Still, there were nice trees along the roads and views toward the mountains to keep things interesting.

Between the heat and 850 feet of elevation gain, this was not an easy 13.62 miles. A beach start doesn't necessarily mean a flat beach run!

Back to the ocean

Finishing back by the water gave me a chance to make my way over the lava rocks for some ocean spray. After the hot roads and hills, getting close to those splashing waves was a welcome way to wrap things up.

Run #50 in the books. Another Hawaiian island, another unfamiliar route explored, and a reminder that headphones aren't just for audiobooks. Only two half marathons left in the 52@52 challenge.

See you next time!
