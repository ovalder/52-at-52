👟 The Runner's Loop (Heel Lock Technique)

The runner’s loop (also known as the heel lock or lace lock) utilizes the extra top eyelets on running shoes to securely anchor your heel without creating painful pressure across the top of your foot.

Why It Works

  • Prevents Black Toenails: Stops your foot from sliding forward on downhills.
  • Eliminates Heel Slippage: Locks your heel firmly into the shoe's heel counter.
  • Reduces Blisters: Minimizes repeated friction against the Achilles.
